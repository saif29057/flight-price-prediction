# Travel documents

## Passport
Many countries require a passport valid for at least 6 months after your arrival date. Check the expiry date before booking.

## Visas
Visa rules depend on your nationality and destination. Apply early: some visas take several weeks. Schengen visas allow travel in 29 European countries for up to 90 days in any 180-day period.

## Travel insurance
Travel insurance covers medical costs, cancellations and lost baggage. It is mandatory for some visas, such as the Schengen visa, with a minimum medical cover of 30,000 euros.
