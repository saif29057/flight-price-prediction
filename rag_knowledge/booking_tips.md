# Booking tips

## When to book
For short and medium-haul flights, prices are usually lowest 3 to 8 weeks before departure. For long-haul flights, booking 2 to 5 months ahead gives the best fares. Last-minute tickets (less than 7 days before departure) are often the most expensive because seats are scarce.

## Cheapest months
Prices rise in the high season: June, July, August and December (summer holidays and end-of-year festivities). January, February, March and November are usually the cheapest months to fly.

## Flexible dates
Moving a trip by one or two days can change the price a lot. Mid-week departures (Tuesday, Wednesday) are often cheaper than Friday or Sunday departures.

## Fare types
A Non-Refundable fare is the cheapest but cannot be reimbursed. A Flexible fare allows changes for a small fee. A Refundable fare costs more but can be cancelled with a refund. Choose a flexible or refundable fare if your plans may change.

## Cabin classes
Economy is the cheapest cabin. Premium Economy offers more legroom for roughly 1.5 to 2 times the Economy price. Business class usually costs 3 to 5 times Economy, and First class even more. On long flights, Premium Economy is often the best value for comfort.
