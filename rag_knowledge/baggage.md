# Baggage

## Cabin baggage
Most airlines allow one cabin bag of about 7 to 10 kg plus a small personal item (handbag or laptop bag). Low-cost airlines often charge for a large cabin bag. Liquids in cabin baggage must be in containers of 100 ml or less, inside a transparent bag of 1 litre.

## Checked baggage
Economy fares on full-service airlines usually include one checked bag of 20 to 23 kg. Business and First class often include two bags of 32 kg. Basic or non-refundable fares may not include any checked bag, so check the fare conditions before booking.

## Excess and lost baggage
Paying for extra baggage online before the flight is cheaper than at the airport. If your bag is lost or damaged, report it at the airline desk in the arrival hall before leaving the airport and keep the report number.
