# Delays, cancellations and passenger rights

## Delays
If your flight is delayed, the airline must provide meals and refreshments after a few hours of waiting, and a hotel if the delay lasts overnight.

## European regulation EC 261
For flights departing from the European Union, or arriving in the EU on an EU airline, passengers can claim compensation of 250 to 600 euros when the flight arrives more than 3 hours late or is cancelled less than 14 days before departure, unless the cause is extraordinary (for example severe weather).

## Cancellations
If the airline cancels your flight, you can choose between a refund and a re-routing to your destination.
