# Airport procedures

## Check-in
Online check-in usually opens 24 to 48 hours before departure. At the airport, arrive 2 hours before a short or medium-haul international flight and 3 hours before a long-haul flight. Check-in desks often close 45 to 60 minutes before departure.

## Security and boarding
Remove laptops and liquids from your bag at security. Boarding gates usually close 15 to 20 minutes before departure. Keep your boarding pass and passport at hand.

## Connections
With one stop, allow at least 1 hour for a connection in the same terminal and 2 hours if you change terminal or pass immigration. A single ticket protects you if the first flight is late; separate tickets do not.
